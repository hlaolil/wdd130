About me

I was born in Leribe in northern Lesotho and grew up in Butha-Buthe. I trained as a pharmacist at the National University of Lesotho and have worked in pharmacy since 2013 — in retail, in a mining clinic, and as a director of two community pharmacies.

Alongside pharmacy I serve in the Assemblies of God Lesotho. In 2021 I planted Revival Fellowship International in Butha-Buthe, and I serve my district as missions director and secretary. I also teach student pastors and lead a small-business training programme for church planters.

Seeing how much time good systems save in a clinic led me to study software development. I now write the tools I use every day: pharmacy records, stock taking, study planning and research instruments.
