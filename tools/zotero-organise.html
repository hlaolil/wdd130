Organise my Zotero library

Adds purpose folders, subject tags and saved searches. Nothing is deleted or renamed.

What it will do
  • 00 Inbox: an empty folder for new items until you sort them.
  • 01 Dissertation: your bi-vocational ministry reading list moves in here.
  • 02 Comprehensive exams: Biblical studies, Theology, Leadership & mission, Research. Each reading is added according to its course.
  • 03 PhD coursework: your eight course folders move in here, unchanged.
  • 04 Teaching & ministry: an empty folder for Global Disciples, RFI and Calvary material.
  • Subject tags on every reading, e.g. Hermeneutics, Luke–Acts & the Spirit, African theology, Leadership, Research: qualitative.
  • Saved searches: Key sources not yet read · To read · Cited in dissertation · Summaries to check.

An item can sit in several folders at once in Zotero, so adding it to an exam folder doesn't remove it from its course folder. It's safe to run again: anything already done is skipped.
