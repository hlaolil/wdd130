Bring my Drive readings into Zotero

Books and articles found in your Google Drive that aren't in your Zotero library yet. You choose; nothing is added until you press Import.

How it works
  • 1. Check my library. Paste your key. The page reads your Zotero library and greys out anything you already have.
  • 2. Look up details. For each ticked reading it asks CrossRef (articles) or Open Library (books) for the proper title, authors, year and journal. A match is only used when the found title really appears in your file; otherwise the row keeps what's listed. Rows whose title had to be taken from the file's first lines say check.
  • 3. Import ticked. Each reading goes into 00 Inbox › From Google Drive with a link to the file in Drive and the tag From Google Drive. Rows marked check also get the tag Check details.

You can change any title, author or type in the table before importing. Running the page again is safe: imported readings show as already in Zotero.
