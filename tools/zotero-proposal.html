File my proposal sources in Zotero

Every work in the reference list of your proposal outline (“Toward a Contextual Theology of Bi-vocational Ministry”), filed by chapter so BookHub shows them where you use them.

What this page does
  • Makes folders inside 01 Dissertation: Ch 2 General literature (with one folder for each section of the chapter), Ch 3 Biblical-theological foundation and Ch 4 Methodology.
  • Works already in your library are moved into their chapter folders (they stay in their other folders too). Works not in your library are added, with the details from your reference list.
  • Every one gets the tag Proposal reference list. Entries your outline marks as still to be checked get To confirm, with what to check written in the item. The six works the outline cites but the reference list leaves out get Add to reference list.
  • Nothing is deleted. Press Check first to see the plan; nothing changes until you press File them.
